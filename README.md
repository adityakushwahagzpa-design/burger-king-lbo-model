# Burger King – LBO Model with DCF

A leveraged buyout model of **Burger King (BKC)** based on the 2010 take-private, with a DCF cross-check and charts.

## Build
- **Transaction assumptions**: LTM EBITDA $444.6m, offer value, entry and exit multiples
- **Capital structure**: USD and EUR secured term loans and senior notes, about **5.9x EBITDA** of total debt ($2.64 billion)
- **Sources and uses** of funds, including refinancing and fees
- Debt schedule with revolver, PIK toggle and circuit breaker switches
- **Case switch** (management and other cases) feeding returns (IRR)
- **DCF** valuation and diluted share count
- **Charts** linked to the LBO sheet, updating with any assumption change
